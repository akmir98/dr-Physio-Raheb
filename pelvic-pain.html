Understanding Pelvic Pain

Pelvic pain is often ignored or undertreated, yet it can significantly affect daily activities and quality of life.

We take a careful, respectful approach to assess pelvic and core function, building a plan tailored to your needs.

Because pelvic pain can stem from several interconnected causes, we take time to understand your full history and daily patterns before recommending any treatment approach.

  • Discomfort during daily movement
  • Pain that affects sitting or standing
  • Tension in the pelvic region
  • Reduced core stability

What Could Be Causing Your Pelvic Pain?

Understanding the root cause is the first step toward lasting relief.

Muscle Tension

Tightness in the pelvic floor and surrounding muscles can cause ongoing discomfort. This tension often develops gradually and can be difficult to identify without a proper assessment.

Postpartum Changes

Pregnancy and childbirth can affect pelvic muscle strength and stability. These changes are a normal part of the recovery process, though they don't always resolve without guidance.

Prolonged Sitting

Extended sitting can place pressure on the pelvic region and surrounding nerves. Making small adjustments to sitting posture and habits can meaningfully reduce this pressure.

Poor Core Stability

Weak core and pelvic floor muscles can contribute to ongoing pelvic discomfort. Strengthening these supporting muscles is often central to lasting relief.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is pelvic pain treatment different for men and women?

The overall approach is similar — improving strength, mobility, and reducing tension — though specific findings and exercises may differ. Assessment is tailored to you individually.

Will I need an internal examination?

Not necessarily. Many aspects of pelvic pain can be assessed externally. Any further assessment is only ever done with your full understanding and consent.

How private is this process?

Completely. Your comfort and privacy are a priority throughout assessment and treatment, and you're always in control of the pace.

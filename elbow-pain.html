Understanding Elbow Pain

Elbow pain is often caused by overuse from sports, exercise, or repetitive work-related movement.

We assess grip strength and forearm mechanics to relieve pain and restore full function.

We look closely at how you grip, lift, and move your wrist and forearm, since elbow pain is almost always connected to how these nearby structures are working — or overworking.

  • Pain when gripping objects
  • Discomfort during lifting
  • Tenderness around the elbow
  • Reduced forearm strength

What Could Be Causing Your Elbow Pain?

Understanding the root cause is the first step toward lasting relief.

Sports Overuse

Sports like tennis and golf place repetitive strain on the elbow tendons. The repetitive gripping and swinging motions in these sports place ongoing stress on the same tendons.

Occupational Strain

Repetitive tasks at work can gradually overload the elbow's tendons. Even everyday tasks like typing or using tools repeatedly can gradually irritate the elbow.

Heavy Lifting

Carrying heavy objects, especially with one arm, can strain the elbow joint. Improper lifting technique shifts more load onto the elbow than the shoulder and back should be sharing.

Poor Technique

Incorrect form during exercise or manual tasks increases stress on the elbow. Correcting form early can prevent a minor strain from becoming a chronic issue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tennis elbow or golfer's elbow?

Both are common forms of elbow overuse injury, differing mainly in which side of the elbow is affected. Assessment will confirm which tendons are involved.

Should I stop playing sport completely?

Not necessarily — we'll help you modify activity rather than stop entirely where possible, while giving the tendon time to recover.

Will a brace help?

A brace can offer temporary relief for some patients, but it works best alongside — not instead of — proper strengthening and movement correction.
